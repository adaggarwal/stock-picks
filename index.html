🔬 About This Model ▶

Trend Reaper is a rules-based quantitative model that ranks equities using a momentum scoring system. The model:

  • Selects the top-ranked equities from each universe based on recent price momentum
  • Uses a trend filter on the broad market index to determine bull or bear conditions
  • Allocates to an equal-weight stock portfolio in bull markets, and shifts to a defensive asset in bear markets
  • Rebalances on a fixed schedule (not in response to daily market moves)

The model is fully systematic — no discretionary overrides are applied. Detailed algorithm parameters are not disclosed.
