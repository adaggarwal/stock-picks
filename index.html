Common Questions

How often do I check?

Only on rebalance dates. Set a calendar reminder so you don't forget.

Do I buy ALL the stocks?

Yes. The strategy works best with all of them in equal amounts. Cherry-picking reduces the benefit.

What if I have more/less money?

Scale proportionally. Just divide your total evenly across all the stocks.

What does "rebalance" mean?

Adjusting what you own to match the latest list. Sell exits, buy new ones, don't touch what stayed.

Stocks are greyed out / says "Watchlist"?

Bear mode is active. Don't buy them. Follow the yellow card instead.
